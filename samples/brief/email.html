TL;DR

The new onboarding flow is ready for a small pilot. We need a decision on the invitation copy and the support owner by Friday. The broader rollout stays on hold until we review what the pilot teaches us.

What changed

New teammates now land on a short setup checklist with a clear first action. The aim is less guessing, faster progress, especially for people joining an existing workspace.

The design notes explain the choices behind the flow. Three changes are ready to try:

  • A shorter welcome: one next step, with supporting detail available when needed.
  • Clearer invitations: the sender and workspace appear together so the invitation has context.
  • A useful finish: a link to the team's current work, instead of another setup screen.

What needs a decision

Two items need an owner before the pilot starts. Please add your input to the decision log by Friday, September 11.

  1. Choose the invitation copy. Maya recommends the shorter version; review both options and flag any missing context.
  2. Confirm the support owner. Jordan can cover the first day, but we still need coverage for the rest of the week.

What happens next

Once those decisions are settled, we will invite five teams and review their feedback after one week. This is a learning milestone, not a public launch date.

The next brief will report where people got stuck, what we changed, and whether the flow is ready for more teams. Follow the pilot plan for scope and timing.
