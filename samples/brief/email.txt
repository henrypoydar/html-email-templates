WHAT CHANGED
New teammates now land on a short setup checklist with a clear first action.
The aim is less guessing, faster progress, especially for people joining an
existing workspace.

The design notes explain the choices behind the flow:
https://example.com/pilot/design-notes

Three changes are ready to try:
- A shorter welcome: one next step, with supporting detail available when needed.
- Clearer invitations: the sender and workspace appear together so the invitation
  has context.
- A useful finish: a link to the team's current work, instead of another setup
  screen.
